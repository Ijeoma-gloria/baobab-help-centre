Understanding your course progress

Understand why your course may not show as complete and what to check next.

Your course progress is based on the requirements of the course, including required activities and assessments. Completing all the learning content does not always mean you have achieved the required passing score.

1

Check required activities

Make sure you have completed all required course activities.

2

Check your Course Quiz score

Review your score on the Course Quiz. Some courses require a minimum score before the course can be successfully completed or a certificate can be requested.

3

Check the passing requirement

If your course requires 80%, a score below 80% means the course requirements have not yet been met.

4

Retake the quiz if attempts remain

If you still have attempts available, review your previous responses before using another attempt.

Used all your quiz attempts? Support can reset your attempts so you can try again.

Need another quiz attempt?

If you have used all available attempts, send Support the course name and quiz details.
