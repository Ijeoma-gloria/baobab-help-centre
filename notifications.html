How to manage your Baobab email notifications

Update the email notifications you receive from Baobab.

Open notification settings
1

Open notification settings

Go to baobabplatform.org/notification_settings while logged in to your account.

2

Update your preferences

Choose the email notification preferences you want to use and save your changes.

Want to stop receiving Daily Digest emails? Select No Email Digest on the Notification Settings page and save your changes.
