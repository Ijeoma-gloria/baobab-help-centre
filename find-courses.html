How to find and access your Baobab courses

Find your courses and continue learning on Baobab.

Open Baobab Courses
1

Log in to Baobab

Log in to your Baobab account.

2

Open Courses

Select Courses from the Baobab navigation.

3

Find your course

Browse available courses or locate the course you are already taking.

4

Open the course

Select the course to open the learning platform and continue learning.

Having trouble opening a course?

Contact Support and include the course name, what happens when you try to open it, and a screenshot of any error message.
