How to change your language on Baobab

Switch the Baobab interface between English and French.

1

Find the language toggle

Log in to your Baobab account and find the EN/FR language toggle in the navigation bar.

2

Select your language

Select EN for English or FR for French. The platform should then display in the selected language.
