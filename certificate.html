How to download your course certificate

Request, view and save a certificate after completing an eligible Baobab course.

Open Baobab courses
1

Open Progress

After you have completed a course, open the Progress tab.

2

Request your certificate

Select Request Certificate. If you do not see this option, check that you earned 80% or higher on all course activities, especially the Course Quiz.

Quiz attempts used up? Quiz resets are handled by Support. Disenrolling and re-enrolling does not reset attempts. Request a quiz reset.
3

View your certificate

Once it is available, select View Certificate.

4

Save it as a PDF

Select Print Certificate and change the print destination to Save as PDF. Certificates display best on a laptop or desktop.

Certificate still unavailable or displaying incorrectly?

Contact Support and include the course name, a description of the issue, and a screenshot of the certificate or error if possible.
