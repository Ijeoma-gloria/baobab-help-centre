Understanding course quizzes and quiz attempts

Learn how quiz attempts and scoring work before you retake a quiz.

Course quizzes assess your understanding of the course content. Keep these points in mind before using another attempt.

  • Quiz attempts are limited.
  • Review your answers before using another attempt.
  • Some questions may have more than one correct answer.
  • If a question asks you to select all answers that apply, you must select all applicable correct answers to receive the point.
  • Changing one answer may not increase your overall score if other questions are still incorrect.
  • Use Show Answers or the available quiz-review feature, where available, to review your responses before trying again.
No attempts remaining? If you have used all your attempts without achieving the required passing score, Support can reset your quiz attempts.
