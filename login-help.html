I can't log in to my Baobab account

Work through these checks if you are having trouble accessing your account.

Open Baobab login
1

Check your internet connection

Make sure your device is connected to the internet. Try opening another website to confirm that your connection is working.

2

Check your email address and password

Make sure you are entering the email address associated with your Baobab account and check for typing errors.

3

Check for an error message

After you try to log in, look for the blue notification banner at the top of the page. If you see “Invalid Id or password.”, check your login details. If you have not reset your password since September 23, select Forgot My Password.

Need to reset your password? See how to reset your Baobab password.
4

Try basic browser troubleshooting

Clear your browser cache and cookies, close and reopen your browser, try another browser, another device, or a different internet connection.

Still need help?

If this did not solve the problem, tell Support what you're experiencing.
