How to change your email address

Update the email address associated with your Baobab account.

1

Log in to Baobab

Log in to your Baobab account.

2

Open Menu

Select Menu.

3

Open Account Settings

Select Account Settings.

4

Confirm your password

Enter your current password when prompted.

5

Edit your email address

Select Edit in the email section and enter your new email address.

6

Verify the new email

Open the verification email sent to the new address and select the verification link to confirm the change.
